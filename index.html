Unit 2　Articles 冠詞 ＆ Nouns 名詞

a不定冠詞 a / an

  • 放在單數可數名詞前面。
    a pen, an apple
  • a 接子音發音開頭的字，an 接母音發音開頭的字（看「發音」不看字母，例：a university, an hour）。
  • 使用時機：
    ① 說的是不特定的人事物　There's a man at the door.門口有一個男人。
    ② 說職業　She's a teacher.她是老師。

b定冠詞 the

可放在不可數名詞、單數／複數可數名詞前：the milk, the pen, the pens

✅ 要用 the 的情況

規則例句
特定的、獨一無二的The man at the door is my uncle. / The sun rises in the east.
海、洋、河、沙漠the Mediterranean, the Atlantic, the Amazon, the Sahara
群島、山脈、複數形國名the Canary Islands, the Himalayas, the USA
姓氏（指全家人）the Smiths（史密斯一家）
民族（指全體國民）the Greeks　but Fiona is Italian.（指個人不加 the）
play ＋ 樂器He plays the piano.
旅館、電影院、報紙the Hilton Hotel, the Rex Cinema, the Times
方位in the east
形容詞／副詞的最高級Tom is the tallest boy in this class.

❌ 不用 the 的情況（零冠詞 –）

規則例句
人名、城市、島、湖、國家、街道、洲、國籍That's Maria. She comes from Spain.
學科、運動History is interesting. We love football.
泛指的不可數名詞／複數名詞I like coffee. Cats like milk.
三餐 breakfast, lunch, dinnerWe have lunch at 1:30.
hospital, home, school, church, bed, work, prison——用在「本來的功能」時The boys are at school.（上學）
but The boys visited their uncle at the hospital.（只是去那棟建築，不是看病）
💡 速記：河、海、洋、沙漠、山脈、群島 → the；湖、單一座山、單一島嶼、城市、單數國名 → 不加（Lake Superior, Mount Everest, Madrid, Japan）。

c名詞 Nouns：可數 ＆ 不可數

名詞可指人、動物、東西、材料、情況、狀態等，分成可數與不可數。

可數名詞 Countable

  • 有單數、複數；單數前可用 a / an，複數前可用數字。
變化規則例字
一般 ＋spen → pens
字尾 s, x, ch, sh ＋esbox → boxes, watch → watches, brush → brushes, glass → glasses
子音 ＋ o → ＋estomato → tomatoes　but piano → pianos, radio → radios
子音 ＋ y → 去 y 加 iesbaby → babies　but 母音＋y 直接加 s：toy → toys
f / fe → vesknife → knives, leaf → leaves　but roof → roofs

不規則複數 Irregular nouns

man → men
woman → women
child → children
foot → feet
tooth → teeth
goose → geese		 mouse → mice
person → people
sheep → sheep
fish → fish
deer → deer　（單複數同形）

不可數名詞 Uncountable

  • 不能數，只有單數；前面不能加 a / an 或數字。
    milk, water, butter, wood, glass（玻璃）, money, time
  • 要表示「數量」時，用量詞：bottle, piece, loaf, slice, carton, cup, glass, packet, kilo, litre, box, bar, bowl ＋ of
    a carton of milk, a loaf / slice of bread一盒牛奶、一條／一片麵包
Unit 3　Some / Any / No / Every ＆ 數量詞

aSome / Any / No / Every

Some、any、no 搭配不可數名詞或複數可數名詞。

字用法例句
some用在肯定句；也用在「提供東西或客氣請求」的問句There's some coffee on the table.
Would you like some milk?
Can I have some water?
any用在疑問句和否定句Are there any books on the desk?
There aren't any books on the desk.
no用在肯定句，但意思是否定（＝ not any）There's no milk in the glass.
＝ There isn't any milk in the glass.
every接單數可數名詞，動詞用單數Every student has got a bag.
💡 some / any 也可當代名詞（後面不接名詞）：Is there any orange juice in the bottle? No, there isn't any.
⚠️ no 本身已是否定，不要和 not 一起用：✗ There isn't no milk.　✓ There's no milk. / There isn't any milk.

複合字 Compounds

SOMEANYNOEVERY
人someone / somebodyanyone / anybodyno one / nobodyeveryone / everybody
事物somethinganythingnothingeverything
地方somewhereanywherenowhereeverywhere
  • 用法和 some / any / no / every 相同，但後面不接名詞。
  • 動詞一律用單數：Everyone is here. Is there anything in the box? There's nobody at home.

bMuch / Many / A lot of / A little / A few

字搭配例句
much、a little不可數名詞There isn't much sugar in the bowl.
There's a little sugar in the bowl.
many、a few複數可數名詞Are there many chairs in the room?
There are a few chairs in the room.
a lot of不可數名詞 ＋ 複數可數名詞 都可以There's a lot of sugar in the bowl.
There are a lot of chairs in the room.

用在哪種句型？

  • much：只用在疑問句、否定句
  • many：通常用在疑問句、否定句
  • a lot of、a little、a few：用在肯定句
💡 代名詞用法（後面不接名詞）：How much money have you got? I haven't got much. / Are there many eggs in the fridge? No, only a few.
💡 口語中可用 lots of 取代 a lot of；代名詞是 lots 和 a lot：There are lots of pens on my desk. There are a lot.

cHow much? / How many?

  • How much ＋ 不可數名詞：問量　How much milk is there in the fridge?
  • How many ＋ 可數名詞（複數）：問數目　How many books have we got?
  • How much is ...?：問價錢　How much is this shirt?

🚨 常見錯誤小抽屜

  • ✗ Every students → ✓ Every student has a bag.（every ＋ 單數名詞 ＋ 單數動詞）
  • ✗ I have many friends.（肯定句較不自然）→ ✓ I have a lot of friends.
  • ✗ She is a French. → ✓ She is French.（個人國籍不加冠詞）
  • ✗ I play the football. → ✓ I play football. / I play the guitar.（運動不加 the、樂器加 the）
  • ✗ Would you like any tea?（提供）→ ✓ Would you like some tea?
